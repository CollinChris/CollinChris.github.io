Context Packer — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 September 2026

Short version: Context Packer collects nothing, transmits nothing, and stores nothing. All processing happens locally in your browser.

What the extension does

When you click Context Packer and choose a pack action, it reads the content of the page you are currently viewing, converts it to Markdown locally, and places the result on your clipboard. That is the entire data flow.

Data collection

  • No data is collected. The extension has no analytics, no telemetry, and no error reporting.
  • No data is transmitted. The extension makes no network requests. Page content never leaves your machine except via your own clipboard, under your control.
  • No data is stored. Packs are not persisted anywhere by the extension; closing the popup discards everything except your clipboard contents.
  • No accounts. There is nothing to sign up for.

Permissions

  • activeTab / scripting — used to read the page you explicitly invoke the extension on. The extension has no access to any other tabs. Its only background behavior is opening the welcome page once, on install; it never reads or monitors pages on its own.
  • clipboardWrite — used to deliver the generated Markdown to your clipboard.

Changes

If a future version changes any of the above (for example, optional paid features that require a licence check), this policy will be updated before that version ships, and the change will be called out in the extension's changelog.
